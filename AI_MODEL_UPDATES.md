## 2026-09-26

### Claude Opus 5.5 — Anthropic (released Sep 22, 2026)
First model in Anthropic's new 5.5 family.
- **Good for:** agentic coding and large-scale code migrations, computer-use automation, long knowledge-work tasks, and biology/life-sciences research (matches or beats Claude Mythos 5.1 in many bio benchmarks).
- **Why it stands out:** ~30%+ faster output, ~40% lower typical workload cost than Opus 5 ($4/$20 per MTok, cache reads $0.20/MTok), and more natural, instruction-following communication style.

### GPT-6 Sol & GPT-6 Luna — OpenAI (released Sep 22, 2026)
Cheaper siblings of the flagship GPT-6 Astra, using the same training approach.
- **Sol — good for:** everyday professional work and complex tasks like coding, where more reasoning is needed, at a lower price than Astra ($2/$10 per MTok).
- **Luna — good for:** high-volume, low-latency clerical work — summarizing documents, extracting information, quick Q&A — at very low cost ($0.10/$0.50 per MTok).
- **Shared improvements:** better factuality, coding, computer use, alignment, and a simplified, less jargon-heavy communication style inherited from Astra.

### Grok 4.7 — xAI (released Sep 21, 2026)
xAI's new frontier model, built on a larger base model than Grok 4.6.
- **Good for:** coding, agentic tasks, and knowledge work requiring long context (500k-token window) and careful multi-step reasoning; text and image input with text-only output.
- **Why it stands out:** works longer on hard problems, self-checks its work more, and has improved safety guardrails. Available in Cursor, Grok Build, the Grok API, and third-party coding tools/routers.

### Gemini 3.8 Flash — Google (released Sep 2, 2026)
Google's most intelligent Flash-tier model to date.
- **Good for:** long-horizon software engineering, autonomous agents, and complex enterprise workflows.
- **Why it stands out:** 1M-token context window, 64K-token output, and customizable low/medium/high "thinking" levels, at the same introductory pricing as 3.7 Flash.
