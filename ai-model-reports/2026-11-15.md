## Quick picks: which model to use for what
- **Hardest coding, reasoning and security research:** Claude Fable 5.2, Gemini 4 Carbon
- **Everyday coding and knowledge work, good price/performance:** Claude Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Hydrogen (if confirmed)
- **Self-hosted or open weights:** Kimi K3.1 Pro (long context, multimodal), Mistral Large 4 (EU), DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (MIT license, Sept, still the top cheap option)
- **Images:** OpenAI Images 2.6. **Video with sound:** Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash. **Robotics and simulation:** NVIDIA Cosmos 3
