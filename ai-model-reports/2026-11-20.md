# New AI Model Releases — Daily Report (2026-11-20)

Covers releases announced roughly **Nov 1 – Nov 20, 2026** (first run of this daily report, so it looks back ~3 weeks).
Benchmark and speed figures are mostly **vendor-reported**; independent results are noted where found.

## Headline releases (biggest news)

| Date | Company (Country) | Model | Open / Closed | What it's good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 18 | Google (US) | **Gemini 4 Neon** | Closed (API) | Mid-tier Gemini 4 model for **coding and agent work**; sits between Hydrogen (fast) and Carbon (deepest reasoning). ~32% cheaper per task than Carbon at high thinking. ~$3 / $15 per M tokens, 1M-token context. |
| Nov 17 | OpenAI (US) | **GPT-6.2 Astra** | Closed (API/ChatGPT) | Frontier model for **hard, long-running professional work** — slides, spreadsheets, documents that follow templates, coding, web design, 3D. $8 / $40 per M tokens (20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra). Independent tests rank it 2nd behind Claude Fable 5.2. |
| Nov 17 | OpenAI (US) | **GPT-6.2 Sol** | Closed (API/ChatGPT) | Cheaper everyday model for **writing, multistep tasks, technical collaboration**. Near Astra on coding benchmark (FrontierCode 2.0) at ~1/5 the cost. $2 / $10 per M tokens. |
| Nov 16 | Alibaba (China) | **Qwen4-Max** | Closed (API-only, unlike Qwen3.8-Max) | Flagship MoE (2.4T total / 71B active), native vision, 1M context. Pitched for **software dev, financial research, industrial engineering** and long autonomous runs. ~$1.50 / $4.50 per M tokens — a low-cost frontier option. |
| Nov 12 | Google (US) | **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** | Closed (API/app) | Fast, everyday model; now powers Gemini app and **Google Search**. Built for **quick agentic workflows**; Google claims 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and 5x fewer fabricated claims. |
| Nov 9 | Anthropic (US) | **Claude Fable 5.2** | Closed (API/apps) | Top general frontier model — independent benchmarks rank it #1; strong at **coding** (FrontierCode 2.0) and hard reasoning. Up to 30% faster and 20% cheaper than Fable 5.1 (~$8 / $40 per M tokens). |
| Nov 9 | Anthropic (US) | **Claude Mythos 5.2** | Restricted | Same model as Fable 5.2, configured for **vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences research** (e.g. 89.6% on CVE Rediscovery eval). Only via verification programs. |
| Nov 9 | Meta (US) | **Muse Ember** | Closed (Meta Model API) | Meta's smallest, cheapest Muse model for **repetitive agent sub-tasks**: tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction, long-document parsing. ~$0.10 / $0.50 per M tokens, 1M context. |

## Quick "which one should I use?" guide

- **Hardest reasoning/coding, money no object:** Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6.2 Astra.
- **Best value for coding/agents:** GPT-6.2 Sol, Gemini 4 Neon, Qwen4-Max.
- **Fast & cheap high-volume agent steps:** Meta Muse Ember, Gemini 4 Hydrogen.
- **Self-host / open weights:** Kimi K3.1 Pro, Tencent Hy4 / Hy4 Flash, ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base, Olala mini (laptops/CPU).
- **Images:** GPT Image 2.6, MiniMax-H3-Image. **Speech-to-text:** ElevenLabs Scribe v3.
