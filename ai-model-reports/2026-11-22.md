## Key takeaways
- **Frontier race:** OpenAI (GPT-6.2), Anthropic (Fable / Mythos 5.2) and Google (Gemini 4) all shipped new flagship models within about 8 days of each other.
- **Security-focused models are a trend:** GPT-6-Cyber and Claude Mythos 5.2 are both gated models for cybersecurity work.
- **China:** Qwen4-Max is API-only. Kimi K3.1 Pro is the strongest open-weight Chinese model. DeepSeek released nothing in November; its latest is V4.1-Flash-1017 from October.
- **Open and sovereign AI:** OVHcloud's Olala shows that Europe is pushing models built to comply with the EU AI Act.
- **Media generation:** Video models now generate audio along with the video (Kling 4.0), and avatar models can hold up for more than 10 minutes.
