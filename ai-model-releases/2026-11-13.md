# New AI Model Releases — 2026-11-13

Daily digest covering roughly **Nov 5 – Nov 13, 2026**. Compiled from web search; a few
tracker sites (llm-stats.com, llmgateway.io, benchlm.ai) were blocked by the session's network
policy, so items are based on news coverage. Benchmark numbers are mostly vendors' own claims.

## Reported but unconfirmed (treat with caution)

- **Gemini 4 Hydrogen / Gemini 4 Carbon (Google)** — trackers list Carbon on Nov 10 and Hydrogen GA on Nov 12 ($1.50/$7.50), but other reports call Hydrogen an unconfirmed leak codename for a fast Flash-tier model; Carbon reportedly limited to select cyber defenders. Google's confirmed GA model remains **Gemini 4 Argon** (Oct 13) — long-horizon reasoning, software engineering, enterprise work.
- **Qwen4-Max-Preview (Alibaba)** — listed by one tracker; no confirming coverage found. Current Qwen line: Qwen 3.8 (27B Apache-2.0 vision model is very popular on Hugging Face).
- **GPT Image 2.6 Flare (OpenAI)** — image generation model listed for Nov 4 by one tracker only.
- **Meta "Muse Ember"**, **HeyGen "Decision 1"** — listed on trackers, no confirming coverage found.

## Quick takeaways
- **Best general model to try:** Claude Fable 5.2 (coding/agents/reasoning).
- **Best open-weights frontier model:** Kimi K3.1 Pro (if you have the hardware).
- **Cheap open MoE:** ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base.
- **Voice / transcription:** ElevenLabs Scribe v3 Live.
- **Trend:** labs are splitting models into a public safeguarded version and a restricted cyber-capable version (Fable/Mythos, GPT-6 Cyber, Gemini 4 Carbon).
