# AI Model Releases — 2026-09-13

Daily roundup of new AI model releases from around the world, and what each one is best used for.

## OpenAI — GPT-6 Astra (Sept 3–4)
OpenAI's new flagship, rolled out first to approved cybersecurity-program users on Sept 3 with general availability the next day. OpenAI calls it the "world's most intelligent and aligned" model, and it meets the "Critical" cybersecurity threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework.
- **Good for:** advanced computer use, software engineering, professional/knowledge work, and science — state-of-the-art across all of these per OpenAI. Available in ChatGPT Plus/Pro/Business/Enterprise and via the OpenAI API and AWS. Pricier than its predecessor at $10/$50 per M input/output tokens (2.5x GPT-5.6 Sol's promo rate).

## Google — Gemini 3.8 Flash & Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber (Sept 2)
Released three weeks after 3.7 Flash, for Google AI Pro/Ultra subscribers; the Cyber variant is limited-access via Google Fairwind for governments/trusted partners.
- **Good for:** software engineering and agentic, multi-step reasoning — on DeepSWE v1.1 it beats most larger frontier models at solving engineering problems end-to-end for a fraction of the cost. The Cyber variant is tuned for vulnerability patching (2.6x more correct Chrome patches than larger commercial models in Chrome Security team testing). Introductory API pricing: $0.75/$3.75 per M tokens through year-end.

## Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.1 & Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1)
Same underlying model, two safeguard tiers: Fable 5.1 is generally available, Mythos 5.1 is gated to trusted-access programs (cybersecurity, life sciences).
- **Good for:** coding, knowledge work, and long-running/agentic problem-solving. Anthropic reports large gains over Fable 5 (52.6% vs 24.7% on a scientific-research benchmark) alongside a 75% cut to cache-read pricing.

## Meta — Muse Spark 1.3 (Sept 2)
Multimodal agentic reasoning model update from Meta AI with a 1,048,576-token context window, available at the same price as 1.2 (contributor tier $0.10/$0.20, standard xhigh tier $1.25/$4.25 per M tokens).
- **Good for:** longer-horizon, multi-workflow agentic coding sessions — it asks clarifying questions, flags when stuck, and confirms before consequential actions instead of hallucinating outcomes. Uses ~20% fewer tool calls and ~25% fewer tokens than 1.2.

## DeepSeek — DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Sept 10)
Smallest model in DeepSeek's new architecture family: an open-weight multimodal MoE with a 552B backbone on a causal encoder-decoder design (8B active params on input, 16B on output), and up to a 1M-token context.
- **Good for:** high-throughput, low-latency multimodal (text + native image understanding) work and long-context document/codebase tasks — DeepSeek says independent tests put it ahead of its own larger V4-Pro, and even edging out Claude Opus 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on Terminal-Bench 2.1 (90.6 vs 89.1 / 88.8). Off-peak pricing as low as $0.003/M cached input tokens.

## Sakana AI — Fugu Max & Fugu Ultra v2.0 (Sept 11)
Not single models but *orchestrators* — one OpenAI-compatible API call that Fugu routes across a pool of underlying models and stitches into one answer. Fugu Ultra v2.0 is the capability-first tier; its model pool was refreshed and now excludes Claude Fable 5/5.1 and GPT-6 Astra.
- **Good for:** teams wanting top-tier multi-model performance without manually routing between providers — Fugu Ultra v2 leads or ties on 5 of 8 benchmarks (incl. DeepSWE, Chartography, Toolathon). 1M-token context, $5/$30 per M input/output tokens; available worldwide except the EU/EEA (pending GDPR compliance).

## Notable trend
Releases this week split along use case rather than one model dominating everything: OpenAI and Google are racing on frontier reasoning/agentic coding, DeepSeek and Sakana are competing on cost-efficient scale and orchestration, and Anthropic/Meta continue pushing into safer, longer-horizon autonomous agents.
