# AI Model Releases — Daily Report, 19 November 2026

Covers releases from about **2–19 November 2026**. Most details come from news coverage and release-tracker sites, not the labs' own release pages. Dates and prices are as reported and may differ slightly between sources.

## Headline releases this week

| Model | Company | Date | Weights | What it's good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gemini 4 Neon** | Google DeepMind | Nov 18 | Closed (API) | Google's mid-tier Gemini 4 model for **complex coding, analytical reasoning and enterprise workflows**. It has a 1M-token context window and is reported #1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. Price: $3 in / $15 out per 1M tokens. |
| **GPT-6.2 Astra** | OpenAI | Nov 17 | Closed (API) | OpenAI's top-quality model for **professional work: documents, slides, coding and long, complex projects**. Context is about 1M tokens. Price: $8 / $40. Early reactions from power users are mixed. |
| **GPT-6.2 Sol** | OpenAI | Nov 17 | Closed (API) | A cheaper model for **everyday work and multi-step agent workflows**. Price: $2 / $10. |
| **Olala mini 7A1B** | OVHcloud (France) | Nov 17 | **Open (Apache 2.0)** | A small mixture-of-experts LLM (7B total, 1B active parameters) that **runs on an 8 GB GPU**. It suits local or on-prem use and European data sovereignty. No quality benchmarks were published yet, and weights were due on Hugging Face around Nov 19. |
| **Qwen4-Max** | Alibaba | Nov 16 (GA) | Closed (API) | Alibaba's flagship for **general reasoning, coding and multilingual work (strong in Chinese)**. Price: $1.50 / $4.50. A preview opened on Nov 11. |
| **Hy4 Flash** | Tencent Cloud | Nov 16 | — | A fast, low-cost model for **high-volume, low-latency** chat and agent tasks. |
| **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** | Google | Nov 12–13 | Closed (API) | A fast, cheap Gemini 4 tier (about 136 tokens/sec on OpenRouter) for **high-throughput and latency-sensitive apps**. |
| **Meta Muse Ember 1.0** | Meta | Nov 9–10 | API | A lightweight model for **repeated agent subtasks: routing, extraction, tool calls and coding steps**. Available via Muse Code and Meta's Model API. |
| **Claude Fable 5.2** | Anthropic | Nov 9 | Closed (API) | A frontier model for **advanced coding, agents and long-context reasoning**. It has a 1M-token context and 128K output tokens. Price: $8 / $40. |
| **Kimi K3.1 Pro** | Moonshot AI (China) | ~Nov 2–6 | **Open weights** (Kimi license) | Widely cited as the strongest Chinese / open-weight model for **reasoning, coding and agentic tool use**. Sources disagree on the exact date the weights went up. |

## Recent context (late October)
- **Mistral Large 4** (Mistral AI, Oct 29): Mistral's largest public model, a European frontier option.
- **DeepSeek:** No new release in November. The current lineup is V4.1-Flash and V4-Pro, and V4.1 Pro has been named but has not shipped.

## Quick picks
- **Best for coding and agents:** Gemini 4 Neon, GPT-6.2 Astra or Claude Fable 5.2.
- **Cheapest capable API model:** Qwen4-Max ($1.50/$4.50) or GPT-6.2 Sol ($2/$10).
- **Run locally or self-host:** Olala mini 7A1B (8 GB GPU) or Kimi K3.1 Pro (needs large hardware).
- **Images and video:** OpenAI Images 2.6, Seedream 5.5, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash.
