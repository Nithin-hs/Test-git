# New AI Model Releases (worldwide): Daily Report for 2026-11-21

Covers releases from **November 1 to 21, 2026**. Benchmark numbers come from the vendors unless noted. Many secondary trackers disagree on dates, so anything I couldn't confirm is marked ⚠️.

## Highlights this week (Nov 15 to 21)

| Date | Model | Company (country) | What it's good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 18 | **Gemini 4 Neon** | Google (US) | Mid-tier model that sits between Hydrogen and Carbon. Built for software engineering, knowledge work and science workflows, with fast end-to-end task completion. Context is about 1M tokens (per one source). Pricing is about $3 in / $15 out per 1M tokens, paid API tier only. Available in the Gemini API, AI Studio, Antigravity and the Gemini app (Pro/Ultra). |
| Nov 17 | **GPT-6.2 Astra** | OpenAI (US) | OpenAI's frontier model for the hardest professional and agentic work. Priced at $8 in / $40 out per 1M tokens, 20% cheaper than GPT-6 Astra. Strong at 3D and visual tasks. Independent coding results are mixed: no gain on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. |
| Nov 17 | **GPT-6.2 Sol** | OpenAI (US) | Everyday model for technical and non-technical work. Available in ChatGPT Work, Codex and the API at the same price as GPT-6.1 Sol. |
| Nov 17 | **HeyGen Avatar 6** | HeyGen (US) | Audio-driven avatar video model with more natural, less repetitive motion. Available through the HeyGen platform and API. |
| Nov 16 | **Qwen4-Max** | Alibaba (China) | Alibaba's flagship model with a 1M-token context. API-only, with no open weights. Priced at about $1.50 in / $4.50 out per 1M tokens. ⚠️ One source still lists it as a preview that runs until Nov 25. |
| Nov 16 | **Hy4 Flash** ⚠️ | Tencent (China) | Fast, low-cost version of Tencent's Hy4 model. |
| Nov 20 | **Kimi K3.1 Pro Prime** ⚠️ | Moonshot AI (China) | Only one tracker lists this model, and I couldn't confirm it anywhere else. |

## Quick guide: which model to pick for what

- **Hardest coding and agent tasks:** Claude Fable 5.2, GPT-6.2 Astra, Gemini 4 Carbon.
- **Everyday work at a good price:** GPT-6.2 Sol, Gemini 4 Neon, Gemini 4 Hydrogen.
- **Very cheap, high-volume agents:** Meta Muse Ember, Gemini 4 Hydrogen.
- **Open weights you can self-host:** Kimi K3.1 Pro, MiniMax H3-Image, NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Nano.
- **Video and avatars:** Kling 4.0 Flash, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, HeyGen Avatar 6, LongCat Video Avatar 1.6.
- **Images:** GPT Image 2.6, Seedream 5.5, MiniMax H3-Image.
- **Speech-to-text:** ElevenLabs Scribe v3.
