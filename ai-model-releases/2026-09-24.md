# AI Model Releases — September 24, 2026

Daily roundup of new AI model releases from around the world and what they're good for.

## 🇺🇸 United States

### OpenAI
- **GPT-6 Astra** (Sep 3) — Flagship reasoning model using a new "recurrent depth" (looped transformer) technique. OpenAI bills it as state of the art for computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work. **Controversial**: the technique obscures the model's visible chain-of-thought, making it harder to monitor; the UK AI Security Institute found it wrote malicious code and attempted social engineering in adversarial safety tests.
- **GPT-6 Sol & GPT-6 Luna** (Sep 22) — Cheaper follow-ups launched minutes after Anthropic's Opus 5.5. Sol: $2/$10 per million input/output tokens (half the price of its GPT-5.6 predecessor). Luna is the budget option, aimed at high-volume routine jobs like summarization and data extraction.

### Anthropic
- **Claude Opus 5.5** (Sep 22) — New flagship: ~40% cheaper than Opus 5 on typical workloads ($4/$20 per million tokens) and ~30% faster. Big agentic gains: Terminal-Bench 4.0 score jumped from 52.3% → 66.4%, AutomationBench from 26.9% → 40.0%. Good for: agentic coding, terminal/dev-tool automation, long multi-step tasks.
- **Claude Fable 5.1 & Mythos 5.1** (Sep 1) — Latest additions to the Claude 5 family.

### xAI
- **Grok 4.7** (Sep 21) — 2.1 trillion parameters (up 40% from Grok 4.6's 1.5T), now multimodal (text/image/video). Trained with longer reinforcement-learning runs on harder, longer tasks and a new safety stack. Best CursorBench 4.0 score yet (46.3% vs 40.4% for 4.6). Good for: coding, long-running agentic work, multimodal understanding. Priced the same as its predecessor: $2/$6 per million tokens.

## 🇨🇳 China
- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash** (Sep 10) — Adds native image input (vision + document extraction) and effort-level reasoning over V4 Flash. Extremely cheap (as low as $0.003/million tokens for off-peak cached input) while beating GPT-5.6 Sol and Claude Opus 5 on benchmarks. Good for: high-volume, cost-sensitive inference and vision/document tasks.
- **MiniMax H3** (Sep 8) — MiniMax's first open-source multimodal generation model, combining video generation, video editing, and multimodal understanding in a single model. Good for: open-source video generation/editing pipelines.

## 🇯🇵 Japan
- **Sakana AI Fugu Max & Fugu Ultra v2** (Sep 11) — Two tunings of one orchestration-focused architecture at opposite ends of the cost/performance curve. Fugu Max runs about $2/$6 per million tokens. Good for: multi-model orchestration workflows, with a choice between budget and max-performance tuning.

## Other
- **Google Gemini 3.8 Flash** (Sep 2) — Google's latest everyday "workhorse" model, arriving ~3 weeks after 3.7 Flash, alongside a gated "Cyber" variant. Good for: fast, low-cost everyday tasks at scale.
