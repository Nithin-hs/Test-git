# New AI Model Releases — Daily Report (2026-11-18)

This report covers models released or announced from about **Nov 1 to Nov 18, 2026**. It was put together from web searches of news coverage and release trackers. Some figures come from secondary or aggregator sources. Those are marked *(unverified)*, so check them against the vendor's own announcement before relying on them.

## Language / general-purpose models

| Model | Company (Country) | Date | Open weights? | What it's good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Qwen4-Max** | Alibaba (China) | Nov 16 | No (API only; smaller Qwen4 models to get open weights later) | Alibaba's most capable Qwen model so far, with about 2.4T total parameters. A general "AI teammate" for work, creative tasks and research. |
| **Hy4 Flash** | Tencent (China) | Nov 16 | No (open-sourcing planned, no date) | A cheap, fast distilled model (224B total / 12B active *(unverified)*). Good for high-volume, low-cost API use. |
| **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** | Google (USA) | Nov 12 | No | Fast, low-cost model for **everyday coding and agentic tasks**. Replaces Gemini 3.8 Flash. Has a 1M-token context and 4 thinking levels, and Google reports about 57% on Terminal-Bench 4.1. Available in the Gemini app, Search, AI Studio and the API. |
| **ERNIE 5.5 Flash Preview** (+ ERNIE-5.5-Flash-Base, Nov 5) | Baidu (China) | Nov 11 | Base weights expected under Apache 2.0 around Nov 25 *(planned)* | A 280B-total / 14B-active MoE with up to 1M context. Balances intelligence with inference efficiency. The API is on Baidu Qianfan. |
| **Muse Ember 1.0** | Meta (USA) | ~Nov 9–10 | No *(unverified)* | A proprietary model with about 1M-token context *(unverified)*, reportedly cheaper than Qwen4-Max. Coverage is thin and no primary Meta source was found. |
| **Claude Fable 5.2** (+ restricted **Claude Mythos 5.2**) | Anthropic (USA) | Nov 9 | No | Technical and knowledge-heavy work: **coding**, enterprise documents and agents. Up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1, with lower API prices. Mythos 5.2 is the same model with different safeguards and is only for vetted organizations. |
| **Olala mini 7A1B** | OVHcloud (France/EU) | Nov 17 (launch event Nov 19) | **Yes** (Apache 2.0, Hugging Face) | A small European-trained 7B model that **runs on a standard CPU** (about 50 tok/s). Comes with EU AI Act compliance docs and a 32K context (128K version coming). Good for local or self-hosted, privacy-sensitive use. |
| *GPT-6.2 Astra* | OpenAI (USA) | Sightings only | No | **Not officially released.** The ID was spotted in Codex and API traffic in early November. The current confirmed flagship is GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3, 2026), which is strong at coding, computer use and cybersecurity. |

## Highlights at a glance
- **Best for coding/agents:** Claude Fable 5.2, Gemini 4 Hydrogen
- **Biggest new flagship:** Alibaba Qwen4-Max
- **Cheapest/fastest:** Tencent Hy4 Flash, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, ERNIE 5.5 Flash
- **Run it yourself (open weights):** OVHcloud Olala mini 7A1B, NVIDIA Cosmos 3 Nano, MiniMax H3-Image (ERNIE 5.5 Flash Base coming soon)
- **Not found:** no new DeepSeek model was found this month.
