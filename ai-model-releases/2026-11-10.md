# AI Model Releases — Daily Report, 10 Nov 2026

## Smaller and niche releases

- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash variants** (DeepSeek, China) — trackers list a new Flash variant on 5 Nov. The last confirmed checkpoint is V4.1 Flash 1017 (17 Oct). Good for fast, cheap general chat and coding. *Unconfirmed.*
- **OpenAI GPT Image 2.6 "Flare" & "Sunburst"** — two image-generation variants listed on 4 Nov. *Unconfirmed.*
- **Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash** (Google) — 4 Nov, the predecessor to 1.2, also for video generation.
- **GLM-5.3 FlashX Legal** (Z.ai, China) — 2 Nov, a fast version of GLM-5.3 tuned for legal work. *Unconfirmed.*
- **Liquid AI LFM3 family** (US) — six open-weight models of about 2B parameters for text, vision, audio and omni tasks, with 256K context. They are built to run **on-device / at the edge** and are free for firms under $10M revenue. Released 27 Oct; trackers list LFM3-1.7B on 6 Nov.

## Quick guide: which model for what
- **Video generation:** Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash
- **Security research / pentesting (vetted orgs only):** GPT-6 Cyber, Claude Mythos
- **Frontier coding and agents:** Claude Fable 5.x, GPT-6 family, Kimi K3.1 Pro (open)
- **Self-hosted open weights:** Kimi K3.1 Pro, GLM-5.3, Meta Muse Glimmer (30B, Apache 2.0)
- **Phones and edge devices:** Liquid AI LFM3
- **Computer-use agents (China ecosystem):** Tencent Hy4
