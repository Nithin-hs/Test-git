# New AI Model Releases — Daily Report (2026-11-25)

Covers releases from roughly **Nov 1 – Nov 25, 2026**, worldwide. Compiled from web search.
Benchmark and pricing figures are mostly vendor-reported or from secondary coverage, so treat them as provisional.
No new major release was found dated Nov 19–25.

## Frontier / general-purpose LLMs

| Date | Model | Company (Country) | What it's good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 9 | **Claude Fable 5.2** (+ Mythos 5.2 for vetted orgs) | Anthropic (US) | Anthropic's top model for the hardest reasoning, long-running agents and coding; better vision; up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1, with lower API prices (reported $8/$40 per M tokens). Opus 5.5 is still the recommended everyday default. |
| Nov 12 | **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** | Google (US) | Fast, cheap everyday model (it replaces the "Flash" tier). It powers Google Search; Google claims it is 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and makes 5x fewer fabricated claims. |
| Nov 16 | **Qwen4-Max** | Alibaba (China) | Proprietary flagship that is only available through the API: 1M-token context; takes text, image and video input; strong on the Artificial Analysis index (~43). |
| Nov 17 | **GPT-6.2 Astra** | OpenAI (US) | High-end professional work: coding, documents, slides, spreadsheets, frontend/browser UIs; uses 22% fewer output tokens than GPT-6 Astra. Priced at $8/$40 per M tokens. Independent tests put it second, behind Fable 5.2. |
| Nov 17 | **GPT-6.2 Sol** | OpenAI (US) | Cheap everyday model ($2/$10 per M tokens) that comes within ~0.5 pt of Astra on coding at ~1/5 the cost. |
| Nov 18 | **Gemini 4 Neon** | Google (US) | Mid-tier model between Hydrogen and Carbon that offers frontier-level reasoning for high-volume workflows. 1M context, $3/$15 per M tokens; a cyber-defense variant goes to security teams via the "Fairwind" program. |

## TL;DR — which to use
- **Hardest coding/agent work:** Claude Fable 5.2 or GPT-6.2 Astra
- **Best value for coding:** GPT-6.2 Sol
- **Fast, cheap everyday use:** Gemini 4 Hydrogen
- **Self-hosted / open weights:** Kimi K3.1 Pro or Tencent Hy4
- **Long-context multimodal (China):** Qwen4-Max
- **Video:** Seedance 2.5 Flash, Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash
