## Which to pick for what

- **Coding and agents (best quality):** Claude Fable 5.2 or Gemini 4 Carbon
- **Coding and agents (cheaper and faster):** Gemini 4 Hydrogen
- **Very cheap, high-volume work (classification, extraction, sub-agents):** Meta Muse Ember
- **Open weights you can self-host:** Meituan LongCat-2.5 (MIT), and Kimi K3.1 Pro once its weights are confirmed
- **Transcription and voice agents:** ElevenLabs Scribe v3 Live
- **Image generation and editing:** GPT Image 2.6 Sunburst (quality) or Flare (speed)
