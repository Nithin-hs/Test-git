# AI Model Releases — 2026-09-12

Daily roundup of new AI model releases from around the world, and what each one is best used for.

## Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.1 & Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1)
Same underlying model, two safeguard tiers: Fable 5.1 is generally available, Mythos 5.1 is gated to trusted-access programs (cybersecurity, life sciences).
- **Good for:** coding, knowledge work, and long-running/agentic problem-solving. Anthropic reports large gains over Fable 5 (e.g. 52.6% vs 24.7% on Terminal-Bench-Science 0.1) at lower cost — cache reads cut 75% to $0.25/M tokens, trimming agentic workload costs up to 45%.

## Google — Gemini 3.8 Flash & Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber (Sept 2)
Released three weeks after 3.7 Flash, for Google AI Pro/Ultra subscribers; the Cyber variant is limited-access via Google Fairwind for governments/trusted partners.
- **Good for:** software engineering and agentic, multi-step reasoning tasks; strong multimodal (text + code + visual) use cases like 3D scene generation and front-end/coding automation. The Cyber variant targets cybersecurity work. Ships with Frontier Safety Framework mitigations (incl. CBRN misuse) and improved prompt-injection robustness.

## Meta — Muse Spark 1.3 (Sept 2)
Agentic coding model update from Meta AI, available via Muse Code and the Meta Model API at the same price as 1.2.
- **Good for:** longer-horizon, multi-workflow agentic coding sessions — it asks clarifying questions, flags when stuck, and avoids hallucinating outcomes. Uses ~20% fewer tool calls and ~25% fewer tokens than 1.3's predecessor, with better resistance to prompt injection.

## DeepSeek — DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash (Sept 10)
Smallest model in DeepSeek's new architecture family: a multimodal MoE with a 552B backbone, 8B/16B active params (input/output), and up to 1M-token context.
- **Good for:** high-throughput, low-latency multimodal (text + native image understanding) applications where cost and speed matter — DeepSeek says it beats its own larger V4-Pro on performance, cost, and latency. Good fit for long-context document/codebase work.

## Sakana AI — Fugu Max & Fugu Ultra v2.0 (Sept 11)
Not single models but *orchestrators* — one API call that Fugu routes across a pool of underlying models and stitches into one answer. Fugu Max is the cost-first tier, Fugu Ultra v2 the capability-first tier (Claude Fable 5/5.1 and GPT-6 Astra are excluded from its pool).
- **Good for:** teams that want top-tier multi-model performance without manually routing between providers — Fugu Ultra v2 leads or ties on 5 of 8 benchmarks tested. Priced at $5/$30 per M input/output tokens (higher above 272K context); API-only, no open weights, not available in the EU/EEA.

## Notable trend
The market is splitting by use case rather than one model winning everything: Gemini for broad day-to-day work, DeepSeek/Sakana for cost-efficient scale, and Meta/Anthropic pushing deeper into autonomous, long-horizon coding agents.
