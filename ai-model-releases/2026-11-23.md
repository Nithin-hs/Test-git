# AI Model Releases Digest: 2026-11-23

Covers about Nov 1–23, 2026. Most benchmark numbers come from the vendors themselves, so treat them as vendor-reported.
I found no new releases dated Nov 21–22. The newest item is Kimi K3.1 Pro Prime (Nov 20).

## Language / frontier models

| Date | Model | Company (country) | Access | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 20 | **Kimi K3.1 Pro Prime** | Moonshot AI (China) | Kimi app/API | A new variant in the K3.1 Pro line. Details are still thin. |
| Nov 18 | **Gemini 4 Neon** | Google (US) | Gemini app, AI Studio API ($3 / $15 per 1M tokens), Antigravity | Mid-tier model for **agentic coding and research**. Google says it is #1 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index (58.6), 65.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.1, and about 3x faster than Gemini 4 Carbon. It has a 1M-token context. A cyber-defense variant goes to vetted security teams through the Fairwind Program. |
| Nov 17 | **GPT-6.2 Astra** | OpenAI (US) | ChatGPT Plus/Pro/Enterprise/Edu, Codex, API `gpt-6.2-astra` ($8 / $40) | Frontier model for **long, hard projects**: professional documents, slides, spreadsheets, agentic tasks. OpenAI reports +5.2 pts on Agents' Last Exam and less than one-third the cost per task of GPT-6 Astra. It shows no gain on coding-agent indexes. |
| Nov 17 | **GPT-6.2 Sol** | OpenAI (US) | ChatGPT, API `gpt-6.2-sol` | Efficient **everyday** model for writing, multistep tasks and technical collaboration. Same price as 6.1 Sol. |
| Nov 16 | **Qwen4-Max** | Alibaba (China) | API only (no weights) | Alibaba's largest model: 2.4T-parameter MoE, 71B active, native vision, 1M context. Positioned as an **"AI teammate" for work and agents**. It scores 43 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Smaller Qwen4 models are promised as open weights. |
| Nov 16 | **Hy4 Flash** | Tencent Hunyuan (China) | Tencent Cloud TokenHub, Yuanbao, WeCom, QQ | **Low-cost, lightweight** model: 224B parameters, 12B active. Tencent says per-task cost is about 1/20 of Hy4. Open-sourcing is planned, with no date yet. |
| Nov 13 | **Gemini 4 Hydrogen** | Google (US) | Gemini app/API | The fast, everyday tier of Gemini 4, for high-volume and low-latency use. |
| Nov 9 | **Claude Fable 5.2** | Anthropic (US) | Claude apps/API | Broadly available general model. Anthropic says output is up to 30% faster than the 5.1 generation. Good for coding, writing and agents. |
| Nov 9 | **Claude Mythos 5.2** | Anthropic (US) | Restricted | Access is limited to vetted organizations doing **cybersecurity and life-sciences research**. |
| ~Nov 2–4 | **Kimi K3.1 Pro** (open weights) | Moonshot AI (China) | Hugging Face, Kimi | Open-weight model aimed at **agentic coding**, with 1M context. Artificial Analysis ranked it #4 (55). It scores 67% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and lands close to Claude Opus 5.5 on coding. The weights were released in early November. |

Recent context: DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 came out on Oct 17 (open, MIT license). I found no new DeepSeek release in November.

## Image / video / audio models (early–mid Nov)

- **MiniMax H3-Image** (China, Nov 10). Open-weight image model that makes 4096×4096 images in under 10 seconds and accepts up to 16 reference images. Its license excludes the US, EU, UK and South Korea.
- **MiniMax H3**. Text-to-image and text-to-video with generated audio and multi-shot video.
- **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash** (Google, GA Nov 5). Fast multimodal generation.
- **Seedance 2.5** (ByteDance). Long-form video storytelling, generation from reference images or clips, and video editing.
- **Wan 3.0** (Alibaba). Generally available video generation.
- **FLUX 3** (Black Forest Labs, Germany, early access). Multimodal visual model with 20-second generations.
- **Lyria 3.5** (Google). Music generation with more realistic vocals.
- **Pika Audio**. Four models covering music, speech, sound effects and soundtracks.
- **Adobe Firefly Audio**. Commercially safe music, voiceover and sound effects.

Dates for the media models come mostly from aggregator pages and have not been checked against the vendors.

## Quick picks
- **Best for coding agents:** Gemini 4 Neon, Kimi K3.1 Pro (open), Claude Fable 5.2
- **Best for office documents and long projects:** GPT-6.2 Astra, Qwen4-Max
- **Cheapest per task:** Tencent Hy4 Flash, GPT-6.2 Sol, Gemini 4 Hydrogen
- **Open weights to self-host:** Kimi K3.1 Pro, MiniMax H3-Image, DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash
