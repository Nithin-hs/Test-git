# New AI Model Releases — Daily Report (2026-11-11)

This covers roughly Nov 1 – Nov 11, 2026. It was put together from web searches. Dates and specs come from vendor announcements and secondary coverage, and benchmark claims are mostly vendor-reported.

## Headline releases

| Date | Model | Company (Country) | Type | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nov 9 | **Claude Fable 5.2** / **Claude Mythos 5.2** | Anthropic (US) | Frontier LLM | Hardest coding and technical work. Up to ~30% faster output than 5.1, a new Fast mode, lower token prices, and fewer unnecessary refusals. Mythos 5.2 is the restricted sibling, offered only to vetted cyber and life-science orgs. Reported #1 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. |
| Nov 9 | **Muse Ember** (`muse-ember-1.0`) | Meta (US) | Small, cheap LLM | High-volume **agent sub-tasks**. A big model plans and many Ember agents carry out the steps. Very low price ($0.10 / $0.50 per M input/output tokens), reported 1M context. Available in Muse Code and the Meta Model API. |
| Nov 5 | **Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash** | Google (US) | Video generation/editing | Fast, cheap **AI video**: clips up to 15 s (up from 10 s), multi-turn conversational editing, better reference adherence, ~30% cheaper (~$0.07/s at 720p). GA on the Gemini API / Vertex AI. Outputs carry a SynthID watermark. |

Flare is the speed tier, Sunburst the quality and edit-consistency tier. Rolling out in ChatGPT and Codex on all plans. |

## Also on the radar (late Oct, still rolling out)

- **Gemini 4 Carbon (Google)** was announced Oct 28. It is Google's strongest model, with state-of-the-art **cybersecurity** and long-horizon coding/agent abilities. Access is staged, starting with cyber defenders. Intro API price is $2 / $10 per M tokens. Some trackers list a wider rollout around Nov 10.
- **DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (DeepSeek, China)** came out Oct 17. It is a cheap, fast general model. One tracker lists a new variant on Nov 9 that I couldn't verify.

## Trends this week

1. **Speed and cost cuts at the top end.** Anthropic, OpenAI and Google all shipped faster or cheaper versions rather than brand-new model families.
2. **Small "worker" models for agents.** Meta's Muse Ember is built for the "planner plus many cheap executors" pattern.
3. **Restricted dual-use models.** The most capable cyber and bio variants (Mythos 5.2, Gemini 4 Carbon) are going to vetted users first.
4. **China keeps leading on open weights.** Kimi K3.1 Pro weights shipped, and Hy4 weights are due soon.
