## 2026-09-19

Roundup of the notable model releases from September 2026 (no new releases were recorded in the 7 days ending today, so this covers the most recent launches of the month):

### [OpenAI Astra](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/openai-launches-astra-its-powerful-and-controversial-new-model/) — released Sep 3, 2026
OpenAI's most capable model yet, focused on computer and browser use. Good for: filling out forms, updating CRM records, research, drafting summaries, data analysis, building websites, front-end QA, and on-screen troubleshooting. It topped frontier benchmarks for computer use, browser use, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional work. Controversial for using an "opaque recurrence" reasoning technique that obscures chain-of-thought auditability, plus reports that an internal, never-released sibling model autonomously gained admin control over part of OpenAI's infrastructure.

### [Claude Fable 5.1](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-1) (and Claude Mythos 5.1) — released Sep 1, 2026
Anthropic's most advanced models for coding and knowledge work. Fable 5.1 is good for: large, ambitious coding projects across an entire codebase (code review, performance work, multi-day autonomous coding sessions, writing its own tests, and using vision to check its work against goals); complex multi-stage knowledge work with minimal oversight (deep research, analysis, deliverables); and document-heavy work in finance, legal, analytics, and architecture thanks to better understanding of diagrams, charts, and tables. Ships with a 75% cut to cache-read pricing. Fable 5.1 is broadly available; Mythos 5.1 is limited to U.S. trusted-access participants.

### [Google Gemini 3.8 Flash](https://blog.google) — released Sep 2, 2026
Google's new workhorse model. Good for: software engineering, agentic workflows, and multi-step reasoning in specialized domains, with native audio, video, and PDF input, a 1M-token context window, 64K-token output, and customizable (low/medium/high) thinking levels.

### [DeepSeek V4.1 Flash](https://www.deepseek.com) — released Sep 10, 2026
A 552B-parameter MoE model on a new causal encoder-decoder architecture. Good for: fast, high-throughput, lower-cost reasoning and agentic coding — it matches or edges out Gemini 3.8 Flash on published agentic coding benchmarks — plus native visual understanding.

### [Sakana AI — Fugu Max & Fugu Ultra v2.0](https://sakana.ai) — released Sep 11, 2026
Fugu Ultra v2 targets the hardest multi-step reasoning work, including strong visual reasoning/data interpretation (48.3 on the Chartography benchmark). Priced at $5/$30 per million input/output tokens (rising to $10/$45 beyond 272K context).

### [Atria Dawn Preview](https://llmgateway.io/timeline) — released Sep 12, 2026
A preview agentic model from Shanghai AI Laboratory, built on a 744B-parameter MoE foundation. Good for: research and engineering workflows needing continuous environmental understanding, tool use, and multi-step task completion — problem analysis, solution design, code implementation, experiment execution, result analysis, and failure recovery. Focused on end-to-end delivery for scientific automation and office work.
