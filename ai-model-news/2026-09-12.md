# AI Model Releases — September 12, 2026

Daily roundup of new AI model releases from around the world and what they're good for.

## OpenAI — GPT-6 Astra (Sept 3)
OpenAI's most capable broadly-deployed model to date. State-of-the-art on computer-use, coding, and science benchmarks (72.6% OSWorld 2.0, 57.9% Terminal-Bench 4.0, 74.1% DeepSWE v1.1), with a 1M-token context window. Good for: agentic computer use, long-running coding sessions in Codex, professional document/slide generation, and defensive cybersecurity work (secure code review, patching). It's also the first OpenAI model to cross the "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold under their Preparedness Framework, so higher-risk offensive tasks (e.g., exploit generation) are restricted.

## OpenAI — GPT-Live 1 (Sept 10) & GPT Image 2.5 Flare (Sept 8)
GPT-Live 1 brings full-duplex voice conversation to the API, able to keep talking naturally while a backend reasoning/agent model works in the background. Good for: real-time voice assistants and agents. GPT Image 2.5 Flare is a faster image-generation variant. Good for: quick, low-latency image generation workflows.

## Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.1 & Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sept 1)
Fable 5.1 is now generally available with a 1M-token context window and up to 128K output tokens. Good for: demanding reasoning, long-running agents, coding, multistep research, and document-heavy professional work. Mythos 5.1 is the higher-capability tier, currently limited to vetted partners in sensitive domains like cybersecurity and life sciences. Both cut cached-token read costs to a quarter of the standard rate.

## Google DeepMind — Gemini 3.8 Flash (Sept 2)
A faster, lighter update in the Gemini 3 family. Good for: cost-efficient, low-latency production workloads where full Gemini 3 Pro/Ultra reasoning isn't required.

## xAI — Grok 4.7 (Sept 12)
Latest iterative update to the Grok 4 line. Good for: general assistant tasks and staying current with xAI's fast release cadence; a dedicated heavy-reasoning variant and coding-focused model are reportedly in development.

## Meta — Muse Spark 1.3 (Sept 2)
A smaller, edge-oriented release from Meta. Good for: on-device/edge deployment and lightweight local workloads rather than frontier reasoning tasks.

## DeepSeek — V4.1 Flash (Sept 10)
Open-weight multimodal Mixture-of-Experts model (552B backbone, 8B/16B active params for prefill/decode) with a 1M-token context, FP4 KV cache, and cross-layer attention reuse for efficiency. Supports a continuously controllable reasoning-effort setting (1–100) to trade cost for accuracy. Good for: teams wanting strong open-weight multimodal reasoning with tunable inference cost, self-hosted at scale.

## Sakana AI — Fugu Ultra v2.0 & Fugu Max (Sept 11)
Fugu Ultra is a reasoning-specialized model that shows a large edge over comparably-sized models on reasoning benchmarks. Good for: complex multi-step reasoning tasks where accuracy matters more than raw speed.

---

**Overall trend this month:** ~11 new models shipped from 7 providers in the first two weeks of September 2026. The frontier race (GPT-6 Astra, Claude Fable/Mythos 5.1) is increasingly about agentic coding, computer use, and cybersecurity capability, while a second track of releases (Gemini Flash, Muse Spark, DeepSeek Flash, Fugu) focuses on efficiency, edge deployment, and cost-per-token — practical workhorse models rather than pure benchmark chasing.

*Note: this summary is compiled from web search of AI news aggregators and vendor announcements at the time of writing and may not be exhaustive or fully verified — treat model names/dates as best-effort, especially for less-established vendors.*
