- **Good for:** self-hosted or low-cost multimodal agentic workloads where open weights and long-context efficiency matter.

## Meta (US) — Muse Spark 1.3 (Sep 2)
Meta Superintelligence Labs' proprietary multimodal reasoning model; 1M-token context, text/image/video input.

- **Good for:** long-running agentic/multi-agent workflows and coding where staying on-task across long threads matters.

## Anthropic (US) — Claude Fable 5.1 & Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1)
Same underlying model shipped under two different safeguard regimes.
- **Fable 5.1** (generally available): leads on agentic coding, knowledge work, and long-horizon problem solving; 75% cheaper cache-read pricing, up to 45% cheaper for highly agentic work vs. Fable 5.
- **Mythos 5.1** (restricted access): same capability, gated to vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences users.
- **Good for:** production agentic coding and long-running knowledge work (Fable), and sensitive dual-use research under tighter access controls (Mythos).

## OpenAI (US) — GPT-6 "Astra" (Sep 3)
OpenAI's most capable model to date; state of the art (per OpenAI) on computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, and science.
- Uses a new "recurrent depth" (looped-transformer) reasoning technique — more efficient, but obscures chain-of-thought, which is drawing scrutiny.
- First model to cross OpenAI's "Critical" cyber-capability threshold (can find/develop exploits for unknown vulnerabilities) — rolled out with added safeguards.
- **Good for:** the most demanding agentic/computer-use and security-research tasks; deploy with care given the reduced reasoning transparency.
