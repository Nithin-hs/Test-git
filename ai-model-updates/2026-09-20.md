# AI Model Releases — 2026-09-20

Daily roundup of new AI model releases worldwide and what each one is good for. September 2026 has seen 13+ new models from 9+ providers; the notable ones from the past two weeks are summarized below, newest first.

## TypeSafe AI — Jev 1.13 (Sep 15–18)
The first "System One" model: instead of generating free-form text, it returns a fast, typed, calibrated decision. Built by Diego Almeida, a co-creator of ChatGPT/RLHF.
- 32K context window, $0.042 per 1M input tokens, effectively free output tokens.
- Claimed up to 100x faster and 100x cheaper than conventional LLMs for the tasks it targets, while staying competitive on intelligence.
- **Good for:** routing, classification, and other in-app decision points where a fast, predictable, structured answer matters more than generated prose.

## Z.ai — GLM-5.3-Flash (unveiled late Aug, widely covered into Sep)
First natively multimodal model in the GLM-5 family (text, image, and video in one pass). Originally seeded anonymously to OpenRouter/OpenCode as "Ox Alpha" before Z.ai confirmed authorship.
- 320B total / 18B active params (MoE), open weights under MIT license, 1M-token context.
- Novel hybrid sparse+linear attention: ~3x lower attention compute and 4x+ smaller KV cache at long context vs. base GLM-5.3.
- Approaches Claude Opus 4.8 on coding/agentic benchmarks at roughly a tenth of the price of GLM-5.2.
- **Good for:** self-hosted or low-cost multimodal agentic workloads where open weights and long-context efficiency matter.

## OpenAI — GPT Image 2.5: Flare & Sunburst (Sep 8)
Two new image-generation API models plus a ChatGPT Images 2.5 refresh across ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex tiers.
- **Flare**: default/faster variant — higher quality than GPT-Image-2 at 50% lower latency.
- **Sunburst**: heavier variant for premium, tightly-controlled edits (campaign creative, product imagery).
- Sharper detail, stronger style adherence, finer edit control, and a new "Sketch" feature that turns drawings into AI images.
- **Good for:** production creative/marketing image workflows needing either speed (Flare) or precision editing (Sunburst).

## Sakana AI — Fugu Max & Fugu Ultra v2.0 (Sep 11)
Orchestration/router models that split work across a pool of open-weight and specialized models and can recursively call themselves.
- **Fugu Max**: $2/$6 per 1M tokens in/out, 40–60% cheaper on output than Sonnet 5, GPT-5.6 Terra, and Kimi K3; best-in-class on Terminal-Bench 2.1, GPQA Diamond, and other agentic benchmarks.
- **Fugu Ultra v2.0**: deeper expert pool, 1M-token context, top/joint-top on 5 of 8 agentic benchmarks; $5/$30 per 1M tokens in/out; hosted API only.
- **Good for:** frontier-level agentic performance without frontier-model pricing.

## Shanghai AI Laboratory — Atria Dawn Preview (Sep 12)
Preview-stage agentic model on a 744B-parameter MoE base.
- **Good for:** end-to-end "office work" agent tasks — deep research, turning questions into executable experiment plans, and scientific-automation workflows.

## DeepSeek — V4.1 Flash (Sep 10)
Open-weights (MIT-licensed) 552B MoE model, 8B active params on input / 16B on output, 1M-token context.
- Matches Gemini 3.8 Flash on agentic coding benchmarks at roughly a third of the price.
- **Good for:** budget-conscious, high-volume agentic coding and tool-calling workloads where open weights matter.

## Google — Gemini 3.8 Flash (Sep 2)
Google's most capable Flash-tier model, built on Gemini 3.7 Flash.
- Runs extra reasoning steps and calls tools iteratively at higher effort levels; 1M-token context; multimodal.
- **Good for:** proprietary multimodal API work with deep Google ecosystem integration and tool use.

## Meta — Muse Spark 1.3 (Sep 2)
Meta Superintelligence Labs' proprietary multimodal reasoning model.
- 1M-token context, text/image/video input; asks clarifying questions on ambiguous prompts and confirms before consequential actions.
- ~20% fewer tool calls and ~25% fewer tokens than v1.2.
- **Good for:** long-running agentic/multi-agent workflows and coding where staying on-task across long threads matters.

## Alibaba — Qwen3.8 27B (Sep 2)
Distilled model released under the permissive Apache License.
- **Good for:** open, self-hostable deployments where licensing freedom and a smaller footprint matter more than frontier scale.

## Anthropic — Claude Fable 5.1 & Claude Mythos 5.1 (Sep 1)
Same underlying model shipped under two different safeguard regimes.
- **Fable 5.1** (generally available): leads on agentic coding, knowledge work, and long-horizon problem solving; agentic scientific research score roughly doubled vs. Fable 5. 75% cheaper cache-read pricing; up to 45% cheaper for highly agentic work.
- **Mythos 5.1** (restricted): same capability, gated to vetted cybersecurity and life-sciences users.
- **Good for:** production agentic coding and long-running knowledge work (Fable), and sensitive dual-use research under tighter access controls (Mythos).

## OpenAI — GPT-6 Astra (Sep 3)
OpenAI's most intelligent and aligned model to date, and the current coding-leaderboard leader on llm-stats.com.
- New state of the art (per OpenAI) for computer use, browsing, software engineering, cybersecurity, science, and professional/document work.
- First model to cross OpenAI's "Critical" cyber-capability threshold — can find and develop exploits for previously unknown vulnerabilities.
- **Controversial:** uses a new "recurrent depth" (looped-transformer) reasoning technique that's more efficient but harder to monitor via chain-of-thought, raising concerns about hidden reasoning and dual-use cyber risk.
- **Good for:** the most demanding agentic/computer-use and security-research tasks — deploy with caution given the concerns above.
